## ✦ Who We Are

**Noveriks** is an independent technology collective focused on turning ideas
into useful, reliable, and accessible digital products.

We bring together developers, designers, creators, researchers, and technology
enthusiasts to explore new ideas, build products, experiment with emerging
technologies, and share knowledge.

Our work spans multiple areas of technology:

- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 💻 Software & Applications
- 🧑‍💻 Developer Tools
- 🌐 Web Platforms
- 📱 Mobile Experiences
- 🎨 Digital Products & Design
- 📚 Developer Education
- 🌍 Open Source
- 💬 Technology Communities

> **We don't build technology for the sake of building technology.
> We build things that are useful.**

---

## ⚡ What We Build

Noveriks is organized around products, experiments, tools, and communities
rather than a single technology or platform.

### 🤖 AI

We explore practical applications of artificial intelligence, including
developer assistance, productivity, automation, and intelligent software.

**Nerivo AI**  
An AI-focused product within the Noveriks ecosystem.

---

### 🧑‍💻 Developer Tools

We build tools designed to make development, experimentation, and everyday
computing more productive.

**DeloQ Downloader - Coming Soon**  
A modern download manager designed for powerful and flexible file
downloading.

**CoreX**  
A Windows system monitoring and optimization toolkit focused on performance,
maintenance, and system management.

---

### 🖥️ Software

We create software products across desktop, mobile, and web platforms.

**Decky Desk**  
Our desktop-focused product for PC STREAM DECK.
---

### 📺 Digital Entertainment

**Movira TV**  
A streaming-focused product exploring a modern entertainment experience.

---

### 👨‍💻 Communities & Developer Education

**Co-Coder**  
A developer-focused community and knowledge platform designed to help
programmers discover programming resources, language tutorials, library
documentation, API references, AI and developer news, and useful tools.
